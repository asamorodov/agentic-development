# A2 mini-dossier cycle reinforcement report

Date: 2026-06-16.

This update fixes a planning weakness noticed after the third natural-Russian rewrite of the A2 target plan. The plan still had the right intention, but the actual mini-dossier instructions could be read too compactly: several points said only to collect a mini-dossier or to collect material through a full cycle. That was too easy for an executor to collapse into one search-and-summary pass.

The target plan now contains an explicit shared cycle for every mini-dossier in P02–P06 and P08–P12:

1. open primary sources;
2. create the first factual draft;
3. record source usage, date, stability, self-description, and analytical role;
4. rewrite the draft in natural Russian;
5. run alternative searches through related wording, links, documentation, changelogs, issues, examples, author materials, and papers;
6. add general material: facts, limitations, examples, neighboring technologies, contradictions, source gaps, and image candidates;
7. reread and rewrite the changed text in Russian;
8. deepen the dossier by its local angles;
9. record limits, failure modes, and where source self-description must not be treated as an analytical category;
10. record visual candidates;
11. add a short note about how the dossier may enter A2 and what should be routed to other Atlas nodes.

Each mini-dossier point now explicitly refers to this cycle and names its local deepening angles. The checks after the first group and after all mini-dossiers also verify that the dossiers were not completed as one shallow pass.

The general Atlas V2 large-article blueprint was updated with a rule that this mini-dossier cycle must not be collapsed into a single sentence when creating future article plans.
