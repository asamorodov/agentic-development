# Chapter VIII target plan individuality update report

Date: 2026-06-15

## Scope

Updated the target-group plan for Chapter VIII after retrospective review. The edit keeps the existing blueprint-derived structure but gives the chapter a stronger individual axis.

## Main changes

- Reframed the chapter from “protected process profiles” to “protected ways of continuing work” in the explanatory Russian text. Technical file paths with `protected_process_profiles` were preserved.
- Added the chapter's central movement: PWG shows where the work is; Chapter VIII shows how to continue it.
- Added `wrong-mode continuation` as the central failure mode: work has state, but the next agent enters it in the wrong role or phase.
- Strengthened the cross-cutting example: a PWG node with several branches, CI signals, review constraints, brownfield investigation and owner decisions.
- Reframed GSD and BMAD asymmetrically: GSD as phase discipline and course recovery; BMAD as role/story/artifact discipline.
- Reframed Gas Town as a boundary to the organizational-operational layer, not a third equal method card.
- Added a double bridge: to Chapter IX through runtime/rights/sandbox, and to Chapter X through long-lived operational organization.
- Replaced broad use of “profile” in Russian prose with “способ продолжения работы”, “процессный подход”, “режим работы” or “методология” where appropriate.

## Not changed

- Chapter texts were not changed.
- The general blueprint was not changed.
- Technical filenames and paths containing `protected_process_profiles` were not renamed.
