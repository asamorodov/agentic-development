Date: 2026-06-15

## Scope

Updated `POST_ATLAS_PER_CHAPTER_TARGET_PLAN_BLUEPRINT.md` so future chapter plans include a short individual tuning step before the full pass queue is assembled.

## Change

The blueprint now asks each plan to formulate the chapter’s own movement in the sequence: what the previous chapter already gave, why it is not enough, what new layer or form of work this chapter introduces, what scene/example should make that visible, which source terms need normal Russian functional explanation, and what question is passed to the next chapter.

The wording deliberately does not require every chapter to have a central failure mode. A chapter may be organized around a failure, but it may also be organized around a transition, comparison, change of scale, new form of state, verification problem, or responsibility boundary.
