# Chapter IX target plan individuality update report

Date: 2026-06-15

## Scope

Updated the target-group plan for Chapter IX after review of its chapter-specific individuality. The change does not alter the chapter text or the general blueprint. It strengthens the plan's own explanatory axis and makes the plan language more natural.

## Main changes

- Reframed Chapter IX as the movement from a chosen way of continuing work to the concrete execution environment in which the agent can act.
- Replaced the weaker “scene assembly” framing with a more precise focus on the agent's real radius of action: reading, local edits, shell commands, browser/devtools, external services, MCP, secrets and production-like boundaries.
- Added a natural-language central failure: technical ability to do something must not be confused with permission for the current phase, with safety, with verification, or with acceptance of the result.
- Strengthened the cross-cutting example: a billing/UI task that moves from local reading and editing to browser checks and possible external calls, where approvals and sandboxing matter.
- Clarified the distinctions among permission, approval, sandbox and authority without pulling Chapter XII into Chapter IX.
- Kept durable execution inside the runtime boundary: it can continue or resume execution, but it does not by itself preserve accepted intent, sufficient evidence, or project authority.
- Rewrote awkward working labels into normal Russian: no “лестница побочного эффекта”, “доступ выглядит как право”, or “документационный ритм” language remains in the plan.
